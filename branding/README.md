# Velide by Baloide

Velide combines the idea of a protective veil with the sound of Baloide. The recommended brand signature is **Velide by Baloide**.

## Logos

- `velide-logo.png`: cobalt/periwinkle V symbol, inspired by a folded veil.
- `baloide-logo.png`: coordinated indigo/periwinkle B monogram.

Both are transparent PNGs without wordmarks, suitable for the app, website, README and avatars. Keep their proportions and clear space. They are raster images; large-format printing requires a vector version. The colors express the brand and do not indicate privacy certification.

The logos are integrated into the shared browser/Electron interface, Windows icons and packages. Original generated assets remain in this folder.
